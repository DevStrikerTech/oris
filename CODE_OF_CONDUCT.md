# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering an open, inclusive, and respectful community for everyone regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in communication.
- Assume good intent and provide actionable feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas, not people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Report issues responsibly and privately when sensitive.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or collaboration.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are inconsistent with this code of conduct.

## Reporting

Report incidents to: `conduct@oris-ai.org` (placeholder).

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with confidentiality maintained where possible.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces and whenever someone is representing the project in public spaces.
